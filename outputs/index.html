Why Colombia + Mexico Now

Colombia +36.3% Fastest-growing market in 2024. Sandbox regulation, growing fintech license approvals, and proximity to US investment appetite.

Mexico: Plata $1.5B Neobank ecosystem maturing rapidly. Series A–B valuations still at LATAM discount (35–45%) despite US-quality metrics.

AI-First Fintechs 25–30% of LATAM fintechs are AI-enabled — higher than US (15%). Early-mover advantage for AI credit scoring, fraud detection.

Valuation Entry Point LATAM Pre-Seed at 55–60% discount vs US. Series A at 35–45% discount. Rare asymmetric entry for GP with global network.
